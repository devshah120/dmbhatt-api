═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Student makes purchase
   ↓
2. Payment verified via Razorpay
   ↓
3. invoiceGenerator.js creates PDF with:
   - Student name, email, phone
   - Product/service description
   - Amount and invoice number
   ↓
4. PDF saved to: uploads/invoices/invoice_xxx_timestamp.pdf
   ↓
5. Record saved to MongoDB (invoices collection)
   ↓
6. Invoice emailed to student via SMTP
   ↓
7. Email status updated in database
